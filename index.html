Firestone Park
Mini Ramp & Pump Track

A free place to ride for all ages: bikes, scooters, skateboards.

Show your support Latest updates

The idea

Friends of Firestone Park won the $150K Akron Parks Challenge for Heintz Hillcrest Park, with “new teen play equipment” on its idea list. We want to build on that: a riding spot where Firestone Park kids, teens and families can skate, scoot and pump.

Plans & ideas

Option A: Mini ramp

A compact ramp that's easy to start on and fun for skaters and scooters. Smaller footprint and lower cost.

Option B: Asphalt pump track

A looping track of rollers and berms for every kind of wheel. Rough estimate: ~$9–17 per sq ft.

Cost examples (estimates)

  • Windsor, CO: about 20,000 sq ft, roughly $250K
  • Canfield, OH: roughly $80K

Proposed site

Near the community center at 1480 Girard St. Not final: Akron Parks still has to review it.

Why it matters

Exercise

Gets kids outside and moving, away from screens.

All ages, all wheels

Bikes, scooters, skateboards: beginners and experienced riders alike.

A first for Akron

As far as we've found, Akron has no public pump track.

Funding ideas

  • Skatepark Project grants: up to $25K, for permanent concrete builds
  • Ohio NatureWorks: applied for through the City of Akron
  • Akron Community Foundation
  • Local business sponsors
  • Community fundraising
